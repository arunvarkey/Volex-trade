Privacy Policy — Volex Terminal

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Volex Terminal is a trading simulator. It uses virtual money and does not place real trades, hold funds, or connect to your bank.

What stays on your device

Almost everything. Your display name and avatar, paper-trading balance, positions and order history, saved strategies, chart drawings, Academy progress, journal entries, risk settings and app preferences are stored locally on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What leaves your device

  • Market data requests to Binance's public price API (api.binance.com). These ask for public prices only. No account or personal information is attached to them.
  • If you sign in, your email address and synced data are handled by Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Signing in is optional — without it the app runs as a guest and nothing is uploaded.
  • Anonymous usage events and crash reports via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These record which screens and features are used, plus diagnostic information if the app crashes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, messages, or your IP address for profiling.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and the app contains no ads.
  • We do not take custody of money. There is none to take.
  • We do not ask for exchange API keys. There is no live trading in Volex, so there is nothing such a key could do here. An earlier version of the app had a screen that collected them anyway; that screen now offers to delete anything it saved on your device.

Photos

If you set a profile picture, the app asks for access to pick one image. That image is stored on your device and is not uploaded unless you are signed in and using profile sync.

Children

Volex Terminal is intended for adults. The app asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before you can use the simulator, and again before you place your first simulated trade. It is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest — no account, nothing uploaded.
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the app.
  • If you have an account and want it deleted, contact us at the address below and we will delete it and any synced data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published at this address.
