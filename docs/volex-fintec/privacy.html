Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide Volex Terminal services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address and User ID (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Financial Data: Purchase history for subscription verification (via Google Play Billing). We do not store payment methods.
  • App Performance: Anonymous crash reports and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics).

2. How We Use It

Your data is strictly used for:

  • Authentication and account security.
  • Verifying Pro/Premium subscription status.
  • Analyzing app stability to fix bugs.

3. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored securely using industry-standard protocols.

Data Deletion Request

We respect your right to be forgotten. To delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Volex Terminal App.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account.

Alternatively, email us manually:

  • Email: support@volexterminal.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Requirement: Include your registered email address.

Requests are processed within 30 days. Deletion is permanent.
